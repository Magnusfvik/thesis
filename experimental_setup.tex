\chapter{Experimental Setup}
\label{chap:setup}

The experiments in this thesis share a common configuration built on the
dataset, model, and metrics introduced in the preceding chapters.
Table~\ref{tab:default_setup} summarises the default values used across all
experiments.  Where an experiment deviates from these defaults, this is noted
explicitly in the relevant section.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Default experimental configuration.}
\label{tab:default_setup}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Category} & \textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Default value} \\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{*}{Dataset}
    & Primary   & AMBAR \\
    & Secondary & MovieLens-1M \\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{*}{Preprocessing}
    & Rating binarisation (AMBAR) & $\geq 3 \to$ liked (5),\; $< 3 \to$ disliked (1) \\
    & Train/test split            & 80/20 per user (temporal if timestamps available) \\
\midrule
\multirow{3}{*}{User sampling}
    & Min.\ training ratings  & 30 \\
    & Min.\ test ratings      & 10 \\
    & Users sampled (AMBAR)   & 5\,000 \\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{*}{CF model (SVD)}
    & Latent factors  & 50 \\
    & Training epochs & 20 \\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{*}{Unexpectedness}
    & Metric            & PURS latent-space Euclidean distance \\
    & Interest clusters & $k = 3$ (k-means on rated item embeddings) \\
\midrule
\multirow{2}{*}{Serendipity}
    & Definition      & Harmonic mean of unexpectedness and normalised relevance \\
    & List length     & Top-10 recommendations per user \\
\midrule
$\alpha$ grid
    & Values tested & \{0.00, 0.15, 0.25, 0.30, 0.35, 0.40, 0.50, 0.65, 0.75, 0.85, 1.00\} \\
\midrule
Reproducibility & Random seed & 42 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
